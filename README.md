# Financial Market Response to Trade Policy Uncertainty

## Project Overview

This research project examines how Indian financial markets responded to trade policy uncertainty, with a focus on equity, gold, and currency markets.

The study analyses the market response of:

- NIFTY 50
- Gold
- USD/INR

## Objective

The objective of the study was to examine how trade policy uncertainty affected returns, volatility, and the relationship between selected financial assets in the Indian market.

## Methodology

The analysis uses daily market data and applies quantitative techniques including:

- Descriptive statistics
- Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) test
- Vector Autoregression (VAR)
- Impulse Response Functions (IRF)
- GARCH(1,1) volatility modelling
- Wavelet analysis

The analysis focuses on different phases surrounding the trade-policy shock to examine both short-term market reactions and volatility persistence.

## Key Findings

The study finds that financial markets adjusted relatively quickly following the trade-policy announcement, while volatility effects persisted for a longer period.

The analysis indicates that:

- Return effects were relatively short-lived.
- Volatility persisted beyond the initial market reaction.
- Different asset classes showed different responses to the trade-policy shock.
- The results provide evidence of changing market dynamics during periods of elevated policy uncertainty.
